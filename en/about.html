Corporate Heritage

About Aileen Agritech Bio

Rooted in Yilan's 50-year agricultural legacy, we bridge traditional farming wisdom with advanced green biotechnology.

Our Origin & Philosophy

Headquartered in Yilan Science Park, Aileen Agritech Bio Co., Ltd. was founded on a deep-rooted commitment to nature and sustainability. Building upon a 50-year family tradition in fruit processing and agricultural heritage, we transform local harvests into premium, scientifically backed skincare ingredients.

Our name, Aileen, reflects our core philosophy: taking from nature while protecting and nurturing it. Like polishing a raw gem into a brilliant jewel, we refine natural agricultural side-streams into high-value bioactive solutions.

Core Competencies

We integrate modern green extraction technologies with rigorous quality control to deliver exceptional standards for our global partners and customers.

Green Extraction

Utilizing low-temperature and eco-friendly methods to preserve maximum active compounds without harmful solvents.

Agricultural Upcycling

Revaluing agricultural side-streams like kumquat peels into high-performance cosmetic assets, driving circular economy.

Certified Standards

Operating under strict ISO22716 / GMP manufacturing guidelines to ensure safety, efficacy, and global compliance.
